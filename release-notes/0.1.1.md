# CueFlow 0.1.1

- The landscape teleprompter fits five lines when screen space allows and uses half the available width.
- Speech following processes transcript updates faster and can accept a one-character recognition error when the preceding words establish your place in the script.
- CueFlow Preview installs alongside the official CueFlow app, with its own scripts and settings.

The signed universal APK supports ARM64, ARMv7, and x86-64. A SHA-256 checksum is included.
